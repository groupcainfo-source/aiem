About Albanian Industry Engineering and Management

AIEM is an international support portal that connects businesses, investors and individuals with the right engineering, business and investment expertise in Albania.

Who we are

AIEM was created around a simple observation: people who want to build, do business or invest in Albania rarely lack ambition. What they lack is a reliable single point of contact who understands the local context and speaks their language. We are that point of contact.

Our team combines engineers, business consultants and project coordinators with a network of licensed legal, tax, medical and real estate partners. You describe your project once, and we bring the right people together around it.

We write to clients in ten languages, so that the conversation happens in the language you think in, and we explain Albanian procedures in terms an international client can act on.

What we are here to do

Our mission is to make decisions about projects in Albania easier and safer: technical and business analysis with visible assumptions, a written scope and price before any work, and specialists who are accountable for their part of the result.

Who we work with

  • Companies and entrepreneurs entering the Albanian market
  • Manufacturers and businesses building or upgrading facilities

Our principles

Honesty about limits

We say what we can do, what we cannot and what depends on others.

Confidentiality

Your project and your data stay private, with a non-disclosure agreement on request.

Transparent terms

Any fee or commission arrangement is agreed in writing in advance.

Accountability

Every request has a reference number and a named contact until it is resolved.

How we work

You describe your request, it reaches the dedicated desk for that service, a specialist replies, and, if the work calls for it, you receive a written proposal before anything starts. Every step is explained on one page.

See how it works

Our boundaries

We are not a law firm, a tax advisor or a dental clinic. Legal, tax and medical matters are handled only by licensed partners. We do not promise outcomes we cannot guarantee, such as the approval of a loan or the return on an investment. The decision always stays yours.
