About AIEM

Albanian Industry Engineering and Management connects businesses, investors and professionals with the right expert in Albania.

We believe good business decisions start with a technical check and honest numbers. That is why our network combines mechanical and electrical engineers with business consultants, and with licensed legal, tax and medical partners where the law requires them.

We work as a support desk. You describe your problem by email, we route it to the specialist who can solve it, and you receive a written proposal before any work starts.

We do not promise outcomes we cannot guarantee, such as the approval of a loan or the profit of an investment. We give analysis with visible assumptions, and the decision stays yours.
