Send your request

Choose the type of support and describe what you need. Your request goes straight to the dedicated desk for that service. The first reply typically arrives within minutes, depending on volume.

What to include in your request

  • What you want to achieve, and by when
  • Where: country, city or site
  • Scale: size, capacity or budget range, if you have one
  • Documents you already have (plans, offers, bills, drafts)
  • Anything that limits the project: regulations, deadlines, partners

What happens next

You receive a confirmation with a reference number. A specialist from the right desk reads your request and replies with a first answer or with the questions we need to give you one. If the work needs a proposal, you receive it in writing with scope, timeline and price.

Your request is treated as confidential. We sign a non-disclosure agreement on request.
