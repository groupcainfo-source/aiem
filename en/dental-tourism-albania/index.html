What dental tourism really involves

Travelling for dental treatment combines a medical decision with a trip. The medical part belongs entirely to the dentist. Our part is everything around it: finding clinics that can be checked, getting a written quote, arranging travel and accommodation, interpreting during visits and keeping the clinic and you coordinated. A well-organised trip lets you concentrate on the treatment instead of on logistics.

How the process works

  1. You send us a short description of your situation and, if you have them, X-rays or earlier treatment records.
  2. We ask partner clinics to review them and to propose a treatment plan and a written quote.
  3. You compare the offers: what is included, how many visits are needed, what the clinic guarantees and what extra costs may arise.
  4. After you choose, we plan the trip: flights, hotel, airport transfers and the visit schedule.
  5. In Albania we can accompany you and interpret at appointments if you wish.
  6. After treatment we stay in contact and help you reach the clinic for follow-up questions.

How to compare clinics without being a dentist

You cannot judge clinical quality from a brochure, but you can check things that tell you how a clinic works:

  • Whether the dentists and the clinic are licensed, and whether they will tell you who will treat you.
  • Whether the quote is written and itemised, and states what is not included.
  • What imaging and diagnosis come before the plan, rather than a price given by telephone.
  • Which materials and brands are used, and whether you receive the details in writing.
  • What the warranty covers, for how long and what you must do to keep it valid.
  • How the clinic handles complications and whether you can reach it after you return home.

Planning the trip realistically

Some treatments need only one visit. Others need several, with healing time in between. For example, when implants are involved, the dentist decides how long to wait between stages, and in many cases that interval is measured in months, which may mean two trips. Ask the clinic for a realistic schedule at the start, and book more days than seem necessary, so that you do not shorten treatment to catch a flight.

Questions to ask before you decide

  • Who will perform each stage of the treatment, and will it be the same dentist throughout?
  • Are there cheaper or less invasive alternatives, and why is this one recommended?
  • What are the risks, and what happens if the result is not as planned?
  • How many visits are needed and how long is each?
  • What does the warranty exclude?
  • Who do I contact after I return, and how quickly?

Aftercare and follow-up

Good treatment includes what happens after you leave the chair. Before you travel, make sure you know the aftercare instructions, the symptoms that need a call to the clinic and how quickly someone will answer. Keep copies of your treatment records, X-rays and the materials used, so that any dentist at home can continue your care. Clinics, hotels and assistance companies that would like to work with international patients can apply to our professional network.

  • Ask for written aftercare instructions in your language.
  • Agree who you contact, and by which channel, if something feels wrong.
  • Plan a check-up with your own dentist after you return.

Frequently asked questions

Are you a dental clinic?

No. We coordinate the process and connect you with partner clinics. Treatment is carried out only by the clinic's doctors.

How do I know what treatment costs?

The clinic gives the price after assessment. We present it in a written quote with any extra costs.

What if I need a check-up after returning home?

Before treatment we ask the clinic about warranty and follow-up checks, and you see this in the quote.

Is dental treatment abroad safe?

Safety depends on the clinic and the dentist, not on the country. That is why we help you check licensing, written quotes, materials, warranty and follow-up arrangements before you travel.

How many days should I plan to stay?

It depends on the treatment. The clinic should give you a realistic schedule, and we advise adding spare days so that nothing is rushed.

Can you arrange hotel and transport?

Yes. We arrange hotel, airport transfers and local transport, and can accompany you and interpret during visits.

What if I have a problem after returning home?

We ask the clinic about warranty and follow-up before you decide, so that you know what to expect, and we help you contact the clinic if you need it.
