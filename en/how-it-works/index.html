How AIEM works: from your request to a written proposal

You describe what you need. We route it to the right specialist desk and reply with a first answer or the questions we need. If the work calls for it, you receive a written proposal before anything starts. Four clear steps, no pressure.

Send your request

The process, step by step

  1. 1

    Describe your request

    Choose the type of support and fill in the request form: your details and a description of what you need. A precise request gets a precise first answer, so it helps to be concrete.

    • What you want to achieve and by when
    • Where: country, city or site
    • Scale and constraints, such as size, capacity or a budget range
    • Documents or drawings you already have
  2. 2

    A specialist replies

    A specialist reads your request and replies, typically within minutes depending on volume. The reply contains either a first answer or the questions we need answered to give you a useful one.

    • A dedicated desk for each service
    • Confirmation with a reference number
    • Reply in your language
    • Clarifying questions, only if needed
    • An early view on feasibility and realistic next steps
  3. 3

    Review a written proposal

    If the work needs a paid engagement, you receive a written proposal: scope, deliverables, timeline, price and what is not included. The first exchange carries no obligation, and you decide at your own pace.

    • Scope and exclusions in writing
    • A timeline with milestones
    • A fixed price or a clearly defined estimate
  4. 4

    Work, delivery and follow-up

    Once you approve, a named lead coordinates the work with engineers, consultants and licensed partners wherever the law requires them. You receive deliverables you can use as they are, and we remain available for follow-up questions.

    • One point of contact for you
    • Licensed partners for legal, tax and medical matters
    • Documents and files handed over in usable formats

What makes a request easy to answer

You do not need technical language or a finished brief. A few concrete facts are enough for a useful first reply:

  • The goal, in plain words
  • The location or site, and the country you are writing from
  • The approximate scale: size, capacity, quantity or budget range
  • Your deadline, if there is one
  • What already exists: plans, offers, bills, drafts, previous work
  • Anything that limits the project, such as regulations, partners or timing

How fast do we reply?

The first reply typically arrives within minutes, depending on request volume. At busy times it can take longer, and the confirmation you receive lets you know your request has arrived. A written proposal takes longer than a first reply, because it has to be accurate. We tell you when to expect it.

What you can expect from us

Clear communication

Plain language, in your language, with a named contact and a reference number for every request.

Written scope and price

Every proposal states what is included, what is not and what it costs, before any work begins.

Confidentiality

Your idea and your data stay private. We sign a non-disclosure agreement on request.

Honest limits

We say plainly what we can and cannot do, and we do not promise outcomes we cannot guarantee.

Ways of working together

The format depends on the service and on what you need. We agree it with you in the proposal.

A single deliverable

A defined result for a defined price, such as a business plan, a design package or a company registration.

A phased project

Larger work split into milestones, so you review and approve each phase before the next begins.

Follow-up support

Continued help after delivery, such as questions during construction, registration steps or partner talks.

AIEM is not a law firm, a tax advisor or a dental clinic. Legal, tax and medical matters are handled only by licensed partners, and we never guarantee outcomes outside our control, such as the approval of a loan or the return on an investment.

Frequently asked questions

Is the first reply free?

Yes. The first exchange is free of obligation. Before any paid work you receive a written proposal with scope, timeline and price.

How fast will I get an answer?

The first reply typically arrives within minutes, depending on request volume. You receive a confirmation as soon as your request is sent.

Can I write in my own language?

Yes. You can write in English, Albanian, Italian, German, French, Spanish, Turkish, Arabic, Russian or Chinese.

Do I need to know exactly what I need?

No. Describe the problem or the goal as you see it. If something is missing, a specialist asks the questions needed to move forward.

How is the price set?

By the scope of the work, which we confirm in the written proposal before you decide. We do not quote a price without understanding the request.

What happens to my data?

It is used only to reply to your request and prepare a proposal. It is not sold. Details are in our privacy policy.

Ready to describe your request?

It takes a few minutes. You receive a confirmation straight away, and a specialist replies with a first answer or the questions needed to give you one.
