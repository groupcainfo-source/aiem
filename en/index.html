Frequently asked questions

What is AIEM?

Albanian Industry Engineering and Management is a support portal that connects you with engineers, business consultants and licensed partners for projects in Albania: mechanical and electrical engineering, business plans, company formation, dental tourism and real estate.

How do I contact you?

Use the request form on any page. Choose the type of support, add your details and describe what you need. Your request goes to the dedicated desk for that service, and you receive a confirmation straight away.

How fast do you reply?

The first reply typically arrives within minutes. At times of high request volume it can take longer.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The first exchange is free of obligation. Before any paid work you receive a written proposal with scope, timeline and price.

Which languages can I write in?

English, Albanian, Italian, German, French, Spanish, Turkish, Arabic, Russian and Chinese.
