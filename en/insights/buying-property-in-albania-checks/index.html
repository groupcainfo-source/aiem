Buying property in Albania: checks before the agreement

Buying property is often the biggest investment a person makes. Checks before the advance payment cost little compared with the problems they prevent.

Ownership and encumbrances

Verify that the seller is the legal owner and that the property has no mortgage, seizure or dispute. This is done with documents from the cadastre registers and with the help of a lawyer.

Permits and legality of construction

Ask for the building permit and the use documents. A building without proper documentation can limit sale, letting or future works.

Technical condition

  • Structure, cracks and damp
  • Electrical and water installations
  • Heating, ventilation and insulation
  • Approximate renovation cost

An inspection by an engineer shows hidden costs before you negotiate the price.

Extra costs

Count transfer taxes, notary services, commissions and renovation. The advertised price is rarely the final cost.

This article is informational and is not legal advice. Consult a lawyer before any financial commitment.
