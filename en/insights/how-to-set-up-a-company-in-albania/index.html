How to set up a company in Albania: the basic steps

Opening a company is the first formal step of any business. A rushed decision on the legal form can cost you later, so it is worth thinking it through before you fill in documents.

1. Choose the legal form

For small and medium businesses the most common choice is the limited liability company (SH.P.K.). For projects that will attract many shareholders there is the joint stock company (SH.A.). There are also the sole trader and the branch of a foreign company. Each form has different liability, capital and obligations.

2. Prepare the founding documents

The statute and the founding act set the name, the object of activity, the capital, the partners and the administrator. The object of activity must match what you will really do, because some activities need special permits or licences.

3. Register at QKB

Registration is done at the National Business Centre (QKB). After registration you receive the business identification number (NUIS), which you need for the bank, taxes and contracts.

4. Open the bank account

The share capital and daily activity run through the company's bank account. Banks ask for identification documents and an explanation of the activity, so prepare them from the start.

5. Set up taxes and employees

After registration the company has obligations for filing and periodic payments, and for registering employees. An accountant who knows your activity saves you mistakes and fines.

This article is informational and is not legal advice. Rules change, so verify with a lawyer or accountant before you act.
