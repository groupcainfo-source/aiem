Privacy policy

Starting template. Before launch, have a lawyer review it against the applicable data protection law.

What data we collect

The data you enter in the request form: your name, email address, phone number, company, country, the description of your request, and the page and language you used. Requests are delivered to our support desk by email through Google services (Google Apps Script) and may be logged in a spreadsheet for follow-up.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, to send a proposal and to improve our service. We do not sell your data.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to our support address.
