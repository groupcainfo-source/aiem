Why checks before the advance payment matter

Property is usually the largest purchase a person or company makes, and in Albania the history of a property can be more complicated than the listing suggests. Decades of changes in ownership rules, informal construction, restitution and compensation claims and incomplete records mean that two apparently identical apartments can carry very different risks. Checks made before you pay cost very little compared with the problems they prevent.

The legal checks we organise with a partner lawyer

  • Ownership. That the seller is the registered owner and has the right to sell, and that the registered details match the property that is shown.
  • Encumbrances. Mortgages, seizures, easements, lease rights and any claims or disputes registered against the property.
  • Cadastral data. That the area, boundaries and registration of the property in the cadastre match reality.
  • Construction legality. The building permit and, where relevant, the documents for use of the building. Properties with informal construction history need special attention.
  • Land and ownership claims. For land in particular, whether there are competing claims from earlier processes.
  • Who may buy. Rules for foreign buyers can differ by type of property, for example between buildings and agricultural land, and are verified before you commit.

The technical inspection

A property can be legally perfect and technically poor. Our engineers inspect what a viewing does not show, and give you a report with approximate renovation costs, so that you can negotiate the price with real figures.

  • Structure: cracks, settlement, damp and the condition of floors, roofs and facades.
  • Electrical installation: panel, wiring, earthing and capacity for the intended use.
  • Plumbing, heating, cooling and ventilation.
  • Insulation, windows and likely energy costs.
  • For commercial and industrial buildings: floor loads, access, utilities and suitability for the planned operation.

Return analysis with visible assumptions

A purchase is also an investment decision. We build a simple model of the expected costs and income: the price, taxes and fees, renovation, furnishing if needed, and then rent, occupancy and operating costs. The assumptions are written down so that you can change them. We show the result under more than one scenario, and we do not promise a return. For larger or more complex projects the same analysis can be extended into a full business plan.

Choosing by purpose

  • Own use. Location, access, services, building quality and the cost of running the property.
  • Long-term rental. Demand in the area, rent levels, tenant profile and management effort.
  • Tourism. Seasonality, regulations for short-term letting and operating cost.
  • Development. Land use rules, permits, utilities, access and construction cost, where engineering input is essential.

Costs beyond the price

The advertised price is rarely the full cost of owning a property. Budget for all of the following before you decide:

  • Transfer taxes, notary fees and registration costs.
  • Legal checks and the technical inspection.
  • Renovation, furnishing and any repairs the inspection finds.
  • Property taxes and building maintenance charges.
  • Management, insurance and vacancy periods if you plan to rent the property out.

Buying through a company

Some investors buy through a company, for tax, liability or business reasons. That decision should be taken before the purchase, because moving a property into a company afterwards can add cost and complexity. If it is relevant to you, we can combine this service with company formation.

What we need from you to start

  • Your purpose: living, renting, tourism, development or business use.
  • Your budget, including how much you plan to spend on renovation.
  • Preferred cities or areas.
  • Whether you will be in Albania for viewings or need help from abroad.
  • Whether you intend to buy as an individual or through a company.

The steps from viewing to signing

  1. Define purpose and budget, then select and view properties.
  2. Request documents for the shortlisted properties.
  3. Complete legal checks and technical inspection before any payment.
  4. Negotiate the price using the findings.
  5. Agree the contract terms, with the lawyer, and sign in the form required by law.
  6. Complete the registration of the new owner and handover.

This service does not replace legal or financial advice. Property law and taxes change, and the decision to invest remains yours. We recommend that no advance payment is made until the legal checks are complete.

Frequently asked questions

Can foreigners buy property in Albania?

Rules depend on the type of property, for example a building or land, and on nationality. We verify them with a lawyer before you pay anything.

Do you guarantee a profit?

No. We give analysis with visible assumptions, not promises of return.

How do you check that a property is free of encumbrances?

By checking documents in the cadastre registers, together with a lawyer, before any advance payment.

Can foreigners buy property in Albania?

Rules depend on the type of property and the buyer. In particular, the position on land differs from the position on buildings. We verify the current rules with a lawyer before you pay anything.

Do you sell properties?

No. We help you find, check and evaluate properties. We do not hold property to sell, which keeps our advice independent.

Do you guarantee a return on investment?

No. We prepare an analysis with visible assumptions and several scenarios, not a promise of return.

Can you help me renovate after I buy?

Yes. Our engineers can prepare the renovation specification, estimate costs and supervise the works.
