Company formation in Albania, from idea to registration

We guide you from the idea to the registered company: choosing the legal form, registration, opening the bank account and setting up the first obligations.

What is included

  • Choosing the form: limited liability company (SH.P.K.), joint stock company (SH.A.), sole trader or branch of a foreign company
  • Drafting the statute and founding documents
  • Registration at QKB (the National Business Centre)
  • Opening the bank account and depositing the share capital
  • Registration for taxes and for employees
  • Initial accounting and a calendar of obligations

Who it is for

For Albanian and foreign entrepreneurs who want to start activity in Albania and need a clear process with one contact person, instead of going from one office to another.

What you receive

  • Legal form recommendation with reasons
  • Founding documents ready to sign
  • Registration certificate and business identification number (NUIS)
  • List of first obligations with deadlines

This service does not replace legal advice. Legal and tax parts are handled with partner lawyers and accountants.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner set up a company in Albania?

In general yes. Conditions depend on the form and the activity. We verify the current requirements before you start.

Do I need to be present in Albania?

In some cases the process can be completed with a power of attorney. We confirm it for your case.

How long does registration take?

Registration itself is fast, but the total time depends on documents and the bank. We give you a realistic calendar at the start.
