Dental tourism in Albania with full coordination

We coordinate your trip for dental treatment in Albania: contact with verified clinics, a written quote, transport, accommodation and interpreting, in one clear package.

What is included

  • Clinic comparison on criteria you can verify: licensing, equipment, documentation
  • A quote with a written treatment plan and visible costs
  • Hotel booking and airport transfers
  • Accompaniment and interpreting during visits
  • Coordination of documents, X-rays and history
  • Support after treatment and contact with the clinic

Who it is for

For patients from abroad, for the Albanian diaspora, and for assistance companies that want a reliable organisation instead of building the trip and the visits themselves.

What you receive

  • Comparison of selected clinics
  • A written quote from the clinic with every extra cost
  • Itinerary: flights, hotel, transfers, visit schedule
  • One contact person during the entire stay

We do not give medical advice. Diagnosis and treatment plan are decided by the dentist.

Frequently asked questions

Are you a dental clinic?

No. We coordinate the process and connect you with partner clinics. Treatment is carried out only by the clinic's doctors.

How do I know what treatment costs?

The clinic gives the price after assessment. We present it in a written quote with any extra costs.

What if I need a check-up after returning home?

Before treatment we ask the clinic about warranty and follow-up checks, and you see this in the quote.
